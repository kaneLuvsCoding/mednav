The original model authors document a combination of DBCLS BodyParts3D, HuBMAP reference organs and MakeHuman source data. The source model geometries, display materials, opacity and selection interactions may be visually transformed by MEDNAV's Three.js viewer. Preserve the attribution in any public deployment and consult the original project for complete dataset/license requirements.

The procedural fallback anatomy meshes are original demo geometry in this project and are simplified visualization, not medical-grade anatomy.
